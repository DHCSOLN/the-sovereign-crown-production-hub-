# SHOPPING AGREEMENT

This Shopping Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of September 16, 2026 (the "Effective Date"), by and between **State of Loc Nation Trust / Dynasty Healing Corp** ("Owner"), and the undersigned producer/production company ("Producer"). 

WHEREAS, Owner owns and controls all rights in and to the original television series concept, intellectual property, open-source visual schemas, interactive digital props, and technical architecture titled ***The Sovereign Crown — Technical Production Hub*** (Library of Congress Control Number: 2025901437; WGA Registered; Compliance Framework tied to Case No. 1:24-cv-00479-RC) (collectively, the "Property").

WHEREAS, Producer desires to obtain the exclusive right to shop the Property to third-party networks, studios, streaming platforms, and financiers (each a "Buyer") for the purpose of securing a development, production, or distribution agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, for good and valuable consideration, the parties agree as follows:

### 1. GRANT OF SHOPPING RIGHTS
Owner hereby grants to Producer the exclusive right, during the Term (defined below), to submit and pitch the Property to potential Buyers. Producer shall act as an independent contractor and has no authority to bind Owner to any agreement or transfer any rights in the Property without Owner’s prior written consent.

### 2. RETENTION OF RIGHTS & OWNERSHIP
Owner retains 100% of all right, title, and interest in the Property, including all underlying copyright authority, narrative logic, technological schemas, ISO 20022 messaging payload models, and structural frameworks. This Agreement is a representation agreement and is **not** a transfer, sale, or assignment of intellectual property. 

### 3. TERM & AUTOMATIC EXTENSION
* **(a) Initial Term:** The exclusive shopping period shall be for a duration of **six (6) months** from the Effective Date, expiring automatically on March 16, 2027 (the "Initial Term").
* **(b) Automatic Extension:** If, upon the expiration of the Initial Term, Producer is engaged in active, good-faith negotiations with a bona fide Buyer for the acquisition or development of the Property, the Term shall automatically extend for an additional **sixty (60) days** (the "Extension Period"). To trigger this extension, Producer must provide Owner with written evidence of active negotiations (e.g., a written term sheet, proposal, or written confirmation of an active offer from the Buyer) prior to the expiration of the Initial Term.

### 4. REVERSION OF RIGHTS
If Producer does not secure a fully executed agreement with a Buyer for the Property prior to the expiration of the Term (including any applicable Extension Period), all rights granted to Producer under this Agreement shall instantly and completely revert to Owner without the need for further documentation. Producer shall immediately cease all pitching, shopping, or utilization of the Property.

### 5. ACQUISITION NEGOTIATIONS
If a Buyer expresses interest in acquiring, licensing, or developing the Property, the final agreement for the underlying rights must be negotiated **directly between the Buyer and the Owner**. Owner retains absolute discretion over whether to accept or reject any third-party offer. Producer’s separate compensation (e.g., a producer fee, back-end points, or attachment as an Executive Producer) shall be negotiated concurrently and paid out of the production budget or by the Buyer, not out of the Owner's personal share.

### 6. CONFIDENTIALITY
Producer agrees to keep all non-public technical layouts, source code, data structures, and narrative materials contained within the Property strictly confidential and shall not disclose them to third parties without securing an industry-standard non-disclosure agreement or prior written consent from Owner.

### 7. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the United States and the applicable statutory copyright laws, keeping consistent with the registered protections of the Property.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties hereto have executed this Shopping Agreement as of the Effective Date.
